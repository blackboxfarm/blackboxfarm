Desktop:
1. Open Super Admin.
2. Click the main tab: `🐋 Whales & MINTS`.
3. Click the inner tab: `👑 KOL Tracker`.

   - `🐦 Twitter` — KOL Twitter scanner.
   - `🕸️ Cabals` — KOL cabal clustering.

Important correction: it is NOT in `Allstars`. The current URL should be `tab=whales-mints`, not `tab=allstars`.
